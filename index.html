Senior Member of Technical Staff at DSO National Laboratories

(Wei Jie) Reiner NG, Ph.D.

I graduated with a Ph.D. in Social Psychology (Nov 2024) from the National University of Singapore. I study attitudes—specifically attitudinal strength—and how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be used to provide clarity on how these cognitive properties inform and guide downstream behaviors in humans and agents over time.

My work combines experimental design, cross-sectional surveys, and social media data with advanced quantitative techniques including network analyses and multilevel meta-analyses in R.

Focus: Cross-Cultural Differences Attitudinal Strength and Persuasion Affective-Cognitive Messaging
